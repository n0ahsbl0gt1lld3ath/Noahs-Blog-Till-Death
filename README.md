My mind is a labyrinth, and not even I know the way out.

Sometimes I wonder what I did to deserve such a stupid life. Who did I anger before death to get to this point. Why should I be dragged down with all the others around me. I know I shouldn't think this way, I'm only 15, but there is no other thought that I can muster other than that. That's a lie, of course I can have other thoughts. Those thoughts, however, are not ones to be said out loud to people I know.

That's why I'm making this blog thing. I'm writing down my thoughts so they stop being in my head. So they're out there into the world, maybe to be found by somebody who feels the same.

Who am I kidding, this isn't gaining traction, and that's the best thing that could happen.
